# Theory of Absolutes

Backend documents for theoryofabsolutes.com — physics, philosophy, science, and eclectic knowledge.

## Purpose

This repo holds the article corpus, research notes, and operating-system docs for the Theory of Absolutes site. Content here is the single source of truth for what appears on theoryofabsolutes.com.

The site currently receives ~1,000 visitors/week. Originally it housed the full range of Thomas's content across all domains. Content is now being split across dedicated sites, with Theory of Absolutes retaining the science, philosophy, and eclectic knowledge articles.
