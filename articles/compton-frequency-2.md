# Compton Frequency

# The Compton Frequency Explained


The **Compton frequency** is the frequency associated with a particle’s **rest mass energy**.


It comes directly from combining:


- Einstein’s mass–energy relation

E=mc2E = mc^2E=mc2

- Planck’s energy–frequency relation

E=hfE = h fE=hf


Setting them equal:


mc2=hfmc^2 = h fmc2=hf


So the **Compton frequency** is:


fC=mc2hf_C = \frac{mc^2}{h}fC​=hmc2​
