# Doubts about the Bible

The comedians, skeptics, doubters, and critics of the Bible have assumed that only the laws and structures of the physical universe exist.  Therefore, all phenomena in the Bible outside of physical phenomenon are either imagination, hallucination, mythology, symbolic, or fabrications.


But, with the hypothesis of a spirit world which is capable of manifesting reality, we have an entirely new set of possibilities of experience of reality. 


Thus, either a spiritual reality that is capable of impacting the physical universe exist, or it does not.
