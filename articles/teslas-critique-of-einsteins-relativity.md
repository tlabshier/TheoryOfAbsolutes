# Tesla's Critique of Einstein's Relativity

**Nikola Tesla on Einstein’s Theory of Relativity**

by Thomas Lee Abshier, ND

4/1/2025


**Thomas Question:**


What was Tesla’s quote about Einstein’s Theory of Relativity?


**AI Answer:**


Nikola Tesla was a vocal critic of Albert Einstein’s theory of relativity, expressing his disagreement in bold and metaphorical language.


Here is the quote:

>
**“The theory of relativity is a magnificent mathematical garb which fascinates, dazzles, and makes people blind to the underlying errors. The theory is like a beggar clothed in purple whom ignorant people take for a king. Its exponents are brilliant men but they are metaphysicists rather than scientists.”**


Tesla believed that Einstein’s theories, particularly the theory of relativity, were overly abstract and disconnected from physical reality. He instead favored a more mechanical and tangible understanding of the universe.


---


### **Context: Tesla’s Criticism**


- Tesla, who was deeply rooted in experimental work and engineering, had a fundamentally different approach to science compared to Einstein, whose work was more theoretical.

- Tesla’s skepticism of relativity came from his belief in the existence of an all-pervasive “ether” as the medium through which energy and light propagate. By contrast, Einstein’s theory dismissed the need for such an ether.

- While Tesla’s critiques of relativity were not widely accepted by the scientific community, they provide insight into the philosophical divide between experimental and theoretical physicists of his time.


---


This quote reflects Tesla’s poetic and dramatic style of expressing his opinions, even when they were controversial or at odds with mainstream scientific thought.
