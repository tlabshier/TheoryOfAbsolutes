## 1. What are ![](https://theoryofabsolutes.com/wp-content/ql-cache/quicklatex.com-3a1595b61c83f13d4a1664966f78e3a3_l3.png) and ![](https://theoryofabsolutes.com/wp-content/ql-cache/quicklatex.com-01f480a4753ea369b8a969a0941636e7_l3.png)?


**Vacuum Permittivity (![](https://theoryofabsolutes.com/wp-content/ql-cache/quicklatex.com-01f480a4753ea369b8a969a0941636e7_l3.png)):**


A measure of how easily an electric field can form in a vacuum.


It determines the strength of the electric field generated by a given electric charge in free space.


**Units:** F/m (farads per meter).


**Vacuum Permeability (![](https://theoryofabsolutes.com/wp-content/ql-cache/quicklatex.com-3a1595b61c83f13d4a1664966f78e3a3_l3.png)):**


A measure of how easily a magnetic field can form in a vacuum.


It determines the strength of the magnetic field generated by electric currents or changing electric fields in free space.


**Units:** H/m (henries per meter).


## 2. How Do ![](https://theoryofabsolutes.com/wp-content/ql-cache/quicklatex.com-3a1595b61c83f13d4a1664966f78e3a3_l3.png) and ![](https://theoryofabsolutes.com/wp-content/ql-cache/quicklatex.com-01f480a4753ea369b8a969a0941636e7_l3.png) Relate to the Speed of Light?


The connection arises from Maxwell’s equations, which describe the behavior of electric and magnetic fields. Specifically:


**Electromagnetic Waves:**


Maxwell’s equations predict that a changing electric field generates a magnetic field, and vice versa. This mutual generation creates a self-sustaining electromagnetic wave that propagates through space.


The equations lead to a wave equation for electric and magnetic fields, with a propagation speed (![](https://theoryofabsolutes.com/wp-content/ql-cache/quicklatex.com-41a04eeea923a1a0c28094a8a4680525_l3.png)) given by:


![](https://theoryofabsolutes.com/wp-content/ql-cache/quicklatex.com-ce418d7d64c5eb3ac98da4abd4aa7825_l3.png)


- ![](https://theoryofabsolutes.com/wp-content/ql-cache/quicklatex.com-41a04eeea923a1a0c28094a8a4680525_l3.png): speed of light

- ![](https://theoryofabsolutes.com/wp-content/ql-cache/quicklatex.com-3a1595b61c83f13d4a1664966f78e3a3_l3.png): vacuum permeability

- ![](https://theoryofabsolutes.com/wp-content/ql-cache/quicklatex.com-01f480a4753ea369b8a969a0941636e7_l3.png): vacuum permittivity


**Physical Meaning:**


![](https://theoryofabsolutes.com/wp-content/ql-cache/quicklatex.com-01f480a4753ea369b8a969a0941636e7_l3.png) determines how the electric field varies in response to charges in free space.


![](https://theoryofabsolutes.com/wp-content/ql-cache/quicklatex.com-3a1595b61c83f13d4a1664966f78e3a3_l3.png) determines how the magnetic field varies in response to currents or changing electric fields.


Together, they set the “stiffness” of spacetime to support electromagnetic waves, dictating the speed at which these waves travel.
