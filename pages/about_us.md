Personal History and Purpose of the Site







Hello, my name is Thomas Lee Abshier, and I practiced as a Naturopathic Physician for 30 years.  I was raised as a Christian, accepted Jesus. and was baptised. In adolescence I was introduced to the Ouija board, reincarnation, channeling, ESP, UFOs, and occult phenomena. As an engineering student at UCLA, I was introduced to Eckankar and astral projection and struggled with the questions about God and religion raised by philosophy.  After college, I went to Naval Nuclear Power School. During my tour in the Navy on board a submarine I had an anxiety attack, which caused me to search for an understanding of why I had experienced that episode.


While doing research at the University of Hawaii Library, I saw a poster advertising a yoga class, which I attended and pursued understanding and reading the Sutras of Patanjali, as interpreted by Christopher Isherwood in his book, “How to Know God.” In 1976, after several months of attending their 3:30 to 6 AM meditation, prayer, yoga, and scripture (Granth Shahib) reading sessions, I took my Sikh vows to become a member of 3HO, a cult founded and led by Yogi Bhajan. I followed that path for 6 years, got married and divorced, went to Naturopathic college, explored a number of the world religions, and used mind-altering chemicals for three years to induce spiritual visions.


Near the end of my training as a Naturopathic Physician, in March of 1987, I had an altered state vision that seemed like a divine revelation, an answer to prayer and search for who was the True God. It was just a single picture of the center of a galaxy surrounded by millions of stars, and a fine thread connecting the center to each star. I interpreted this vision as the relationship of God to each particle of creation. This concept gave me an idea about how God had manifested the universe from His consciousness.


In a series of insights that came to me over the next several months, I saw the stories of the Bible in a new light.  I saw how the Father created the Son, by duplicating Himself, by looking back at Himself from an imagined position of “other.” I saw how the Son, used that same Son used that same strategy to create innumerable Conscious Points, which each had various properties, and combined to make the quanta of mass and energy. I saw how the Son formed and evolved mass to manifest life, and eventually  why God had worked through history, culminating in the crucifixion of Jesus Christ. That insight gave me the rationale I needed to confidently proclaim my allegiance to Christ and accept the validity of the Bible as a revelation of God’s Word and Way.


The insight about how God may have created the universe from His consciousness was a powerful seed concept. With my science background, I began to look for clues as to how God might have created the universe.  This theoretical journey has continued to the present, and the Theory of Absolutes, the body of work presented in the Physics portion of this site is my contribution to the consideration of how the universe was constructed, and functions.


At this point in my life, I am confident that the world would operate in the most ergonomic, peaceful, pleasant, and satisfying way if every person chose to actively pursue incorporating Biblical Christianity into his/her life.


The scope of the influence of the Way of Christ encompasses all aspects of life, including both the animate and inanimate world.  It is for this reason that this site attempts to examine how the Way of Christ affects all fields/aspects/domains of the human experience.


The Political Blog, which was once a site I called DrSenator.com, is an examination of events, laws, judgments in the group realm.


The Psychological Blog was once a site I called MyCounselor.com and DoctorCounselor.com.  I gained many insights into the nature of human interactions by my 30 plus years as a Christian Counselor – a role that was a natural extension of my work with patients who came to me with physical ailments.  The psychological realm is where we live, it is the place where we experience life.  We just want to be happy in our relationships and our personal experience of life.  The way of Christ is the method we must follow to experience that desired joy and peace of life.  Thus, in that body of psychological work, I seek to give practical advice into how to live life in His Way in various life situations. 


In all the areas of life considered on this site, I seek to show how God’s rules bring order, satisfaction, and fairness in our experience of life. 


The physics exploration connects physics with metaphysics, and shows how God is at the foundation of the creation. 


The Theology section shows how His Way is superior to all other theories and teachings, but compatible with all which speak and live the same principles.  


In the Political section, I seek to show that His principles must be incorporated and guide the group action, law, and judgment. 


In the Medicine section, I seek to show that His hand guided the creation of the bodies of both man and woman.  And, that nature, His creation, speaks to His Laws and provides the tools of healing.


The purpose of this site is to give tools to those who want to consider a rational, full spectrum witness to the reality of the Lordship of Jesus Christ for themselves; or, to prepare your own mind to give an argument and add persuasive force to your words in your effort to win the soul of a loved one; or, to point your loved one to a resource that may help them examine their own life, by comparing their world view with a testimony that validates the efficacy of surrendering their life to the Way and Truth of Christ.
